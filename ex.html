Quick Guide: How to Create Your First Schedule
  • Schedule File (CSV Recommended): Upload a CSV file or Image file (like a timetable export or screenshot of your work/class hours) so the AI knows when you're busy.
  • Goals: Describe what you want to achieve and your ideal pace with commas in between to add more goals (e.g., "Train for a 10k" or "Study COMP 1510 1 hr/day").
  • Target Date: Fill in day of the week for your deadline after by: (e.g., by: Friday) so tasks scale realistically over time.
